# Use case: read-only research

An agent retrieves enterprise information, compares sources, or produces an explanation without changing the target system.

## Required controls

- Identify the principal and permitted data scope.
- Resolve tenant, source, environment, and retrieval route.
- Record retrieval time, source identifiers, query or filter scope, and citations.
- Distinguish source content from the agent's interpretation.
- Mark stale or inaccessible sources explicitly.

Verification checks that material came from the intended source and that the answer preserves uncertainty. Successful retrieval does not prove that a source is current, complete, or authoritative for every question.
