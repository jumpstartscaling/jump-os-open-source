# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, useful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, explain disagreements with evidence, respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and protect private or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private infrastructure, and deliberate misrepresentation of evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the project issue tracker without publishing private information. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the community.
